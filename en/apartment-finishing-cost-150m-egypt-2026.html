Value per Square Metre

Why 150 m² Gives Better Value per m² Than a Smaller Unit

  • Fixed costs are spread wider. The kitchen, bathroom fittings, main electrical panel and armoured entrance door cost about the same in 100 m² and 150 m² — so their share of each square metre falls.
  • Bulk material orders. Larger quantities of porcelain, paint, cable and piping improve supplier pricing and reduce the waste ratio.
  • Labour efficiency. Crews set up once and work across larger connected spaces, so daily productivity rises.
Budgeting

How to Allocate a 150 m² Finishing Budget

  • Infrastructure first. Plumbing and electrical works are approved and pressure- and circuit-tested before any ceilings or flooring start — later changes mean breaking finished work.
  • Everyday essentials second. Flooring, doors, kitchen and bathrooms take the largest share after infrastructure.
  • Decoration last. Complex gypsum, wallpaper and decorative lighting can be added later without demolition.
  • Keep a 10% contingency for site surprises and design changes, and never downgrade electrical quality to save money.
Cost Control

Five Ways to Lower the Cost Without Lowering Quality

  • Use one flooring material across most spaces to cut waste and gain a volume discount.
  • Keep complex gypsum to the reception and use clean flat ceilings in the bedrooms.
  • Reserve natural marble for the entrance and reception and use high-quality local porcelain in the rooms.
  • Freeze the design before buying anything — early purchases end in shortages or mismatched finishes.
  • Sign an itemised contract with brands, grades, a staged schedule and a defined warranty.

How long does it take to finish a 150 m² apartment in Egypt?

Finishing a 150 m² apartment typically takes 4 to 6 months on the Standard or Premium package and 6 to 8 months on Luxury or Super Luxury, depending on design readiness and the amount of decorative work. The schedule is fixed in the contract; compressing it shortens natural curing stages and causes cracking in plaster and paint.
