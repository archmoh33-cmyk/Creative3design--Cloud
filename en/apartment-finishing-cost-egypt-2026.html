What the Price per m² Actually Includes

The most common reason two quotations differ in Egypt is scope, not rate. Here is our full scope.

  • 3D interior design and VR walkthrough before a single wall is built
  • Complete execution drawings (electrical, plumbing, joinery, ceilings)
  • All civil, finishing, electrical and plumbing works
  • Certified materials, supplied and installed

  • A written contract with a fixed schedule and stage-based payments
  • Quality assurance handover and after-handover service
Cost Drivers

Five Factors That Move the Final Number

  • Finishing tier — the single biggest driver, roughly a 3× spread between Standard and Ultra VIP.
  • Built-up area — larger units lower the cost of design per m² but raise the total.
  • Unit condition — a bare-shell unit costs more than one delivered semi-finished by the developer.
  • Joinery and built-ins — kitchens, dressing rooms and wall cladding concentrate a large share of the budget.
  • Location and access — delivery logistics in New Cairo, Sheikh Zayed or the North Coast differ from central Cairo.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does apartment finishing cost per square meter in Egypt in 2026?

Apartment finishing in Egypt costs from EGP 5,500 per m² for the Standard package up to EGP 17,000+ per m² for Ultra VIP, as of July 2026. Premium is EGP 6,000/m², Luxury EGP 10,750/m² and Super Luxury EGP 14,250/m². All rates include 3D design, execution drawings and full site supervision.

What is the total cost of finishing a 100 m² apartment in Egypt?

A 100 m² apartment costs EGP 550,000 with the Standard package, EGP 600,000 Premium, EGP 1,075,000 Luxury, EGP 1,425,000 Super Luxury and EGP 1,700,000+ Ultra VIP, based on the published July 2026 rates per square meter.

What is included in the finishing price per square meter?

Every package includes 3D design and execution drawings, certified materials, all civil and finishing works, electrical and plumbing works, daily on-site engineering supervision, a written contract with a fixed schedule, quality assurance and after-handover service.

How long does finishing an apartment take in Egypt?

A 100 m² apartment typically takes 8 to 10 weeks, 150 m² takes 10 to 14 weeks and 200 m² takes 14 to 18 weeks. The schedule is fixed in the contract and depends on the package level and the state of the unit at handover.

Can I pay for apartment finishing in installments?

Yes. Payment is stage-based: 30% on contract signing, 30% mid-execution, 30% before handover and 10% after final inspection.

Do you work with clients who live abroad?

Yes. We work with expatriate and overseas owners through 3D visuals, VR walkthroughs and weekly progress reporting, with an English-speaking team based in Cairo. Remote design for Gulf clients starts from SAR 8/m² for architectural and SAR 12/m² for interior design.
