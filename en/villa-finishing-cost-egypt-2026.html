Villa vs Apartment

Why a Villa Costs More — and It Is Not the Rate

The rate per square metre is identical for a villa and an apartment at the same finishing level. What raises a villa's total is scope an apartment rarely carries:

  • Internal staircase — structure, cladding, balustrade and lighting.
  • Facades — stone, cladding or GRC across multiple elevations.
  • External works — landscaping, driveway, boundary wall, external lighting.
  • Pool and plant room — pumps, filtration, drainage.
  • More wet areas — typically 4–6 bathrooms versus 2–3.

These are quoted separately from the per-metre finishing rate, and any honest quotation says so explicitly.

Scope

What the Rate Includes — and What It Does Not

Included: interior design with 3D visualisation and a VR walkthrough, execution drawings, materials and labour for the agreed scope, and engineering supervision through to handover.

Not included: furniture, appliances, landscaping, pools, boundary walls, and any structural modification requiring a permit. Every included item is listed line by line in the written contract before work starts.

A 350–400 m² villa typically takes 5 to 9 months. The schedule belongs in the contract with a defined clause for unjustified delay — a timeline without one is a moral commitment, not a contractual one.

Published rates run from EGP 5,500/m² for Standard to EGP 17,000+/m² for Ultra VIP, inclusive of design and engineering supervision. A 400 m² villa starts at about EGP 2,200,000 at Standard level.
